Free vs paid models — Variant B (chosen 2026-07-30)

Pain point: the provider card said KOSTENLOS or NUTZUNGSBASIERT as if cost were a property of the provider — but Gemini has both: Flash has a free tier (20 requests/day), Flash Lite a generous one (500/day), and Pro has no free quota at all (0/0 without billing). Decision: grouped model list (Variant B) in Settings, tier-grouped picker W2b in the chat. Variants A/C and the discarded picker options were removed from this file 2026-07-30. Prerequisite: registry gains per-model free/freeQuota and Gemini Pro is corrected to free: false.

B

Grouped model list

The dropdown becomes a visible radio list with two labelled groups: “Free tier” (with live quota meters fed by GET /api/quota-cooldowns + usage) and “Requires billing”. The provider card drops its cost badge entirely — cost lives only in step 2. Providers whose models are all in one group show a single group header.

Pros

  • The free/paid split is structural, not a badge — impossible to misread.
  • Live quota meters answer “how much do I have left today?” right in the picker (data already exists for the quota badges).
  • Provider cards get simpler, not busier — the misleading provider-level badge disappears.

Cons

  • Biggest diff: replaces the dropdown with a new radio-list component in the settings flow.
  • Takes more vertical space — with many models the Model tab scrolls.
  • Quota meters need the usage endpoint wired into the settings modal (extra fetch + refresh logic).
