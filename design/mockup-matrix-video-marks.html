Video marks on Matrix — three ways to make them readable

Transcript and chapter marks paint with the five fixed PDF pastel constants (bg-[#FEF08A] …). On the PDF that is correct — the page is white paper in every theme. The video pane's surface follows the theme, so on Matrix a mark is a light pastel block under light phosphor text: measured in the running app, #b7e8bc on #FEF08A = contrast 1.2:1. The chat solved this exact problem on 2026-07-26 via the --syflo-hl-* variable overrides.

All three variants change Matrix only; the four light themes keep today's pastels untouched. Sample text is the real mark from the makemore chat.
