Delete confirmation — restructuring the body text

Chat titles are often generated from long or messy source text (a paper abstract, a video transcript line). Quoted inline inside one run-on sentence, the modal turns into a wall of text and the actual question — what happens if I click delete — gets buried. This affects one shared modal (chat delete and category delete both render through it), so a fix here covers every delete confirmation in the app.

Three ways to restructure the same information. All keep the same two facts: what gets deleted and what happens to it — just organized differently.

0 CURRENT — baseline for comparison
The title is quoted inline inside a single flowing sentence. With a short chat title this reads fine; with a long or garbled one (as in the reported case) the quote and the consequence blur into one unbroken block.
