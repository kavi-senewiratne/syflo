  • Removed: the chevron button (branched-from-toggle), the dropdown panel (branched-from-dropdown), the branchQuoteExpanded state, and its outside-click/Escape handlers — nothing to open or close, and no separate "dropdown file" to reason about.
  • Kept: the overflow measurement (branchQuoteOverflows via ResizeObserver) — the tooltip is a no-op and skipped entirely when the quote already fits on one line, so short quotes get no hover affordance they don't need.
  • Fixes the underscore look: the link drops border-bottom/underline entirely — color is the only "this is clickable" signal now, so there's nothing left to render as a stray line under the quote marks, math or not.
  • Content stays rich: the tooltip renders the same <InlineMarkdown text={chat.parent_word} /> as before, so KaTeX math in the quote still renders correctly inside it — a native title attribute couldn't do that.
  • Keyboard/touch: tooltip also opens on focus (:focus-within), so keyboard users tabbing to the link see it too; clicking/tapping still navigates as before.
