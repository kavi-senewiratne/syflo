The date sections under one “Timeline” parent

The sidebar now has three top-level things: Pinned, the user’s categories, and then five or six loose date headings — Today, Yesterday, This week, Last week, This month, Older. Those six are one idea wearing six labels, and they cannot be put away together.

The proposal: gather them under a single parent with its own symbol, so the entire clock-side of the sidebar closes in one click and the categories get the screen.

Decided and BUILT, 2026-08-16. §01 → B: the parent is an 11 px heading like “Pinned”, the date headings indent under it, the chats do not move. §02 → clock, and the word is “Verlauf” in German / “History” in English. Strengthening 1 only: the parent heading is gray-500, one step darker than its children; the indent stays at 28 px. §03-3 resolved itself — with the parent staying a heading, Pinned stays a heading too, and the top level keeps one grammar. Separately: the folder-plus button was removed from the header; creating a category now starts in a chat’s right-click menu.
What this changes. Nothing about how chats are grouped — the date sections are computed exactly as they are today. It changes only the hierarchy above them: six siblings become one parent with six children. The parent is not a category: it cannot be renamed, deleted or filed into, and it exists whether or not the user does anything.
The question underneath all three variants is a number: where the chat titles start. Today an uncategorised chat’s title begins at 10 px. If the date sections become children, the honest reading is that their chats indent to 28 px like a category’s do — and that is most of the sidebar moving right. The dashed line in each frame marks it.

§01 · Three ways to make the date sections children

Same content in all three: Pinned, one category, then the timeline. Watch the dashed line — it marks where the chat titles begin, and it is the whole cost of the idea.

§02 · The symbol

Three Lucide candidates, in the row form. All three are gray-400 like the folder and the pin, so every theme remaps them.

1 Clock
Says “when”, matches the words Today / Yesterday exactly. The most literal, and the most easily confused with the queued-question clock already used on chat rows.

3 History
A clock with a backward arrow — says “what came before”, which is what the section actually is. Busiest of the three at 13 px; the arrow can smear at gray-400.

The word matters as much as the mark. Timeline is your word; History is what most apps call this and what a new user would look for; By date says what it does and reads as a setting rather than a place. Say which you want — the string lives in strings.ts in both languages (“Timeline” / “Verlauf” / “Nach Datum”).

§04 · What this costs in the code

Small, if it stays cosmetic

  • No schema change. The date sections are computed in groupChatsByDate.ts and stay untouched.
  • One more entry in the syflo.sidebarClosedSections set that already holds pinned and date:Today — the collapse machinery exists.
  • Two strings in strings.ts, one icon, one nesting level in the render.

Large, if the chats indent (variant A)

  • The chat row already takes an indented flag, so the change is one argument — but it applies to most rows in the sidebar, and every screenshot, every theme check and the syflo-math-fade truncation on long titles wants re-checking.
  • The mindmap and the expanded tree view are unaffected — they never show date sections.

Worth deciding once

  • If Timeline is a row that cannot be renamed or deleted, its right-click menu is empty. Either it has no menu at all, or it gets one entry nobody needs.
  • Dropping a chat on Timeline should mean “remove from category” — it is the only sensible reading, and it is free to add.
As built.
  • The parent exists only while it has something to head. With every chat pinned or filed there is no “Verlauf” row — not an empty one.
  • Closing the parent hides its date sections; it does not reset them. Each keeps its own key in syflo.sidebarClosedSections, so a section closed on purpose is still closed when the timeline comes back (§03-2, as shown).
  • The nested date headings drop the reserved icon slot — their indent already says what they are, and an empty slot on top of an indent spends the same 16 px twice.
  • Verified in the running app: 16 chats collapse to one row reading “VERLAUF 16”, and reopening returned “Gestern” open with the four sections the user had closed still closed.
Still open — not built.
  • No right-click menu on the parent. It cannot be renamed, deleted or filed into, so it has no verbs; a menu with nothing in it is worse than none.
  • Dropping a chat on the parent could mean “remove from category”. Free to add, but nobody has asked for it yet.
  • Keyboard (ADR-0011): headings are still not focus items, here as elsewhere.
